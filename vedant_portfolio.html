About me

I didn't find design.
I fell into it.

After graduating with a BE in Computer Engineering, I spent four months trying to become a full-stack developer. I built things in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React. The results never matched the effort. So instead of grinding harder at something that wasn't clicking, I did something harder — I stopped and looked around.

Explored cloud, Linux, data analytics. Nothing fit. Then I found UI/UX design — and realised I'd been doing pieces of it my whole life. Back in diploma, I was always the one deciding how the screens should look, how the flows should work. I just didn't know that was a job.

"Once I found the field, I stopped waiting for permission to start."

Four projects later — including a real internship, research with actual users, and a redesign that my mom tested herself — I'm a beginner who works like someone with something to prove.

2026

DOX — Task Management Tool

"Every tool drowned me in features. I designed one that doesn't."

Built during my internship at Amdox Technologies. I started by using Jira, ClickUp, Linear, and Trello as a first-time user — and immediately felt the problem. Too many features, no clear path. DOX fixes that.

→ Clean 6-item navigation — only what a new user actually needs from day one
→ Extension system — AI, integrations unlock when you need them, not before
→ 30+ screens delivered — web, mobile (2 versions), landing page, onboarding
→ Full component library + AMOLED-aware colour tokens

02

UX Research · Redesign
2024

UTS App Redesign

"My mom asked me to help her book a ticket. She couldn't do it alone. That became this."

The UTS app has millions of daily users — but people like my mom rely on others to use it. After talking to her and my neighbours, I found the real problem: it wasn't a bad UI. It was fear.

→ Primary research — interviewed real non-tech users, not guessed their problems
→ New feature designed — first-time video booking guide popup, not just a redesign
→ FAB button for re-accessing guide — existing users aren't left behind
→ Decluttered homepage — reduced visual anxiety for first-time users
View case study

App Design · Case Study

Wash Buddy

"Hostel students have zero time for laundry. I built the app that does it for them."

A laundry and ironing service app connecting hostel and PG residents with nearby service providers. Problem statement sourced from a UI/UX design course — but the research, design, and case study were entirely mine.

→ Competitive research — analysed existing laundry apps for gaps and patterns
→ Full UX process — user flows, sitemap, wireframes, high-fidelity screens
→ Tier 1 + 2 city focus — designed for the actual market, not an assumed one
→ Published case study on Behance — complete with research documentation
View case study
04
First Project
UX Research · App Redesign
2024

Library App Redesign

"I assumed the problem. The users corrected me. That was the most important lesson I learned."

My first ever UX project — built from a fictional brief to practice the process. I thought bad UI was driving users away. A survey told me I was wrong. The real problem was a broken search algorithm.

→ Assumption proven wrong — started with UI hypothesis, research revealed search algorithm failure
→ User survey conducted — real data over designer intuition
→ Redesigned search experience — relevance filters, priority ranking, clearer results UI
→ First lesson learned — your intuition is just a starting point. Users tell you the truth.
